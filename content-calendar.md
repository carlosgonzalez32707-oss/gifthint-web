# GiftHint Content Calendar — Months 4–6

**Strategy:** Each post targets a high-intent, low-to-medium difficulty keyword
in the gifting space, links internally to at least one `/gifts/[occasion]`
landing page, and contains one `<InlineCTA />` mid-article to capture readers
who are ready to act.

Posts publish on Tuesdays. Month 6 (Christmas content) should be live by
**1 October** to catch early-October shopping intent.

## Publishing Checklist (per post)

Before marking a post `published: true`:

- [ ] Frontmatter complete: `title`, `date`, `excerpt`, `occasion` (or `null`), `readTime`, `published: true`, `hasAffiliateLinks`
- [ ] `<InlineCTA />` placed after the correct section
- [ ] All internal links resolve (`/gifts/[occasion]` paths verified)
- [ ] Affiliate disclosure flag set correctly — `hasAffiliateLinks: true` only for posts with product links
- [ ] Post renders correctly at 375px mobile width
- [ ] Related posts sidebar shows ≤ 3 posts and doesn't include this post
- [ ] Email capture form renders at the bottom of the post
- [ ] Run Lighthouse on the published URL — LCP < 2.5s, CLS < 0.1
- [ ] Submit URL to Google Search Console → URL Inspection → Request indexing
